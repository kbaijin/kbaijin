## About

## Selected Research

### Visual Understanding

- **[FGOS-Net](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.28503)** — *ECCV 2026 · First author*<br>
  Thin structures are easily fragmented by conventional state-space scans. FGOS-Net introduces geometry-aligned serialization to preserve structural connectivity with efficient inference.<br>
  [`Paper`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.28503) · [`Code`](https://github.com/kbaijin/FGOS-Net) · [`ECCV`](https://eccv.ecva.net/virtual/2026/poster/5356)

- **[IP-SAM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.27250)** — *ECCV 2026 · Second author*<br>
  Prompt-conditioned segmenters fail when deployment prompts are absent. IP-SAM generates intrinsic prompts for automatic segmentation across camouflage and polyp datasets.<br>
  [`Paper`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.27250) · [`Code`](https://github.com/zhang1huiyao/IP-SAM) · [`ECCV`](https://eccv.ecva.net/virtual/2026/poster/5354)

### Scientific AI Agents

- **[TerraWeave](https://kbaijin.github.io/#terraweave)** — *ICLR 2027 under review · First author*<br>
  TerraWeave learns policies and reusable skills from verified execution state, enabling more reliable credit assignment in long-horizon Earth-observation workflows.

- **[EarthArena](https://kbaijin.github.io/#eartharena)** — *ICLR 2027 under review · Co-first author*<br>
  EarthArena evaluates real-world scientific agents beyond answer-only scores, using paper-grounded investigations and rubrics over answers, artifacts, and execution.
