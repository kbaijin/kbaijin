I am a postgraduate student in Computer Technology at the [Technology and Engineering Center for Space Utilization, Chinese Academy of Sciences (CSU)](http://english.csu.cas.cn/) and the [University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (UCAS)](https://www.ucas.ac.cn/). My work spans large language models, multimodal foundation models, computer vision, AI agents, and lunar remote sensing.

Since April 2026, I have also worked with [THUNLP](https://nlp.csai.tsinghua.edu.cn/) and Qiyuan Lab on remote-sensing foundation models and intelligent agents, in collaboration with Zonghao Guo. I am actively seeking Ph.D. positions beginning in Fall 2028.

目前我在中国科学院空间应用工程与技术中心和中国科学院大学攻读计算机技术硕士，研究大语言模型、多模态基础模型、计算机视觉、智能体与月球遥感。自 2026 年 4 月起，我与清华大学 THUNLP 和启元实验室合作开展遥感基础模型与遥感智能体研究，合作导师为郭宗浩老师；我正在寻求 2028 年秋季入学的博士机会。
